# Golden outputs of dw2d: the default (deterministic) route and the legacy route of dw2d 0.1.5

Since 0.8.0 the default route is `deterministic=True`: the cocircular cells of Qhull's triangulation are re-triangulated by a fixed rule (a fan from a vertex chosen by SHA-256 of the cell's integer coordinates), triangles and dual-graph edges are listed in canonical order, and the watershed sorts edges stably. `dw2d_deterministic_route_outputs.{npz,json}` hold its golden outputs; the files below, of dw2d 0.1.5, are the golden master of the legacy route, `deterministic=False`.

`dw2d_deterministic_route_outputs.npz` holds, per case, the mesh vertices and edges, the watershed seeds, the edge scores, the triangles and the label of each triangle after the watershed; the `.json` holds the DCEL angles, lengths, curvatures and cell areas at full precision, and the versions used. They were recorded on macOS arm64 (Python 3.14.6, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1, scikit-image 0.26.0, networkx 3.7) by `record_the_deterministic_route_outputs.py`. `test_deterministic_route.py` checks on every machine, without skipping any case, that the default route reproduces them bitwise (DCEL quantities to 1e-12 relative) and that the stored x86-64 triangulation gives the same outputs as the machine's own Qhull. Against the legacy golden, two meshes differ, doublet_03 and doublet_08 at `min_dist` 3: in each, the rule takes the other diagonal of one cocircular cell on the interface (3 segments differ; one interface length changes by 0.059 % and 0.052 %; no angle changes). Canonical triangle order, canonical edge order and the stable sort change none of the 18 meshes.

## The legacy route (dw2d 0.1.5)

`dw2d_0.1.5_on_scipy_1.13_outputs.{npz,json}` were produced by the unmodified dw2d 0.1.5 (commit `2a5b578` of its former repository, VirtualEmbryo/delaunay_watershed_2D, with `scipy.interpolate.interp2d`) and foambryo2d (`42324c5`) on Python 3.12.3, NumPy 1.26.4, SciPy 1.13.1, scikit-image 0.22.0 and networkx 3.2.1, by a recording script kept in the development repository. Two runs were bitwise identical.

The `.npz` holds, per case, the mesh vertices and edges returned by `return_mesh()`, the watershed seeds and the edge scores. The `.json` holds, per case, the SHA-256 of every intermediate stage, the DCEL quantities (angles, lengths, curvatures, cell areas) and the foambryo2d tensions of the seven modes, at full float precision, and the errors the legacy code raised.

Cases: the C. elegans mask shipped with foambryo2d, `mask_Cells_1` and `mask_Cells_3`, and `mask_Cells_2` with `expansion_labels=6`, at `min_dist` 3 and 5; ten exact equilibrium doublets (`../data/exact_doublet_masks.npz`, parameters in `../data/exact_doublet_parameters.json`) at `min_dist` 3.

`dw2d_0.1.5_on_scipy_1.13_triangulations.npz` holds, per case, the Delaunay triangles of that run (int32, each row's vertices sorted, rows in lexicographic order). Qhull's triangulation of dw2d's integer points is not unique where four or more points are cocircular, and it differs between machines there. The triangles were recovered on macOS arm64 by enumerating every triangulation of the cocircular polygons of Qhull's output and keeping the one whose hash equals the x86-64 one. They reproduce this run's `tesselation_points` and `graph_edges` stage hashes (the Delaunay edge set) in all 18 cases, which `test_reconstruction_on_the_golden_triangulation_is_the_legacy_one` checks. Qhull's row order of the triangles was not recorded and is not needed: on the 18 cases the mesh does not depend on it.

What the golden tests pin on which machine: given these triangles, everything downstream is bitwise the golden run on every machine; the end-to-end comparisons are bitwise wherever Qhull returns these triangles (Linux x86-64 and arm64), and are skipped where it does not (13 of the 18 cases on macOS arm64), after an exact integer check that Qhull's triangulation differs from these only inside cocircular polygons.

## The border-edge orientation (0.8.0)

Since 0.8.0 the edges along the image border are oriented like every other edge (material `m1`, the medium, on the left of `v1 -> v2` in `(x, y) = (col, row)` under the rule of that time; on its left in `(row, col)` under the rule of the last section); dw2d 0.1.5 left them in an arbitrary order. The legacy golden files are not changed: `test_golden_master.py` compares the legacy route with them after reversing exactly the legacy border edges that break this orientation (`legacy_edges_with_the_border_orientation_fixed`), every other edge bitwise, and compares the half-edge quantities with those of the legacy mesh so corrected. Only `mask_Cells_2` with `expansion_labels=6` has such edges (212 of 634 at `min_dist` 3, 130 of 380 at `min_dist` 5).

## The relocation default (0.8.0)

Since 0.8.0 the default route relocates the junctions (`relocate_junctions=True`), so `dw2d_deterministic_route_outputs.{npz,json}` hold the relocated vertices; when they were regenerated for this default, only the vertex positions (and the half-edge quantities that depend on them) changed, on all 18 cases: edges, seeds, scores, triangles and triangle labels are those of the plain deterministic route. The legacy golden master is compared with `relocate_junctions=False`.

## The half-edge loops (0.8.0)

Since 0.8.0 a boundary loop that the half-edge structure reverses to orient it is reversed with its half-edges' endpoints (`reverse_closed_line`); dw2d 0.1.5 reversed the order of the half-edges only, so `compute_lengths` and `compute_curvatures`, which walk the faces, cut most interfaces one edge off their junctions, and `compute_lengths` dropped the last edge of a closed loop without junction. The mesh, the angles and the cell areas do not depend on it (bitwise unchanged on every golden case). `test_golden_master.py` therefore compares the legacy route's angles and cell areas with the 0.1.5 record, its lengths with the sum of the legacy mesh's edge lengths, and its curvatures with nothing; `dw2d_deterministic_route_outputs.json` was regenerated for this fix (only `lengths` and `curvatures` changed).

## The orientation rule (0.8.0)

Since 0.8.0 every edge is stored the other way round: each edge is the positively oriented boundary of its first material in the index frame `(row, col)`, dw3d's rule for triangles (material `m1` on the left of `v1 -> v2` in `(row, col)`, where it was on the right). `dw2d_deterministic_route_outputs.npz` was regenerated for it, by `record_the_deterministic_route_outputs.py` with the versions recorded in the `.json` (Python 3.14.6, NumPy 2.5.4, SciPy 1.18.1, scikit-image 0.26.0, networkx 3.7, macOS arm64), and checked against the former file: on the 18 cases, every edge row is the former row with its two vertices swapped (2 221 edges), and every other array is bitwise the former one; the `.json`, which holds the half-edge quantities, is byte for byte unchanged. The legacy golden files of dw2d 0.1.5 are not changed: `test_golden_master.py` compares the legacy route with them after reversing every legacy edge (and, first, the border edges that 0.1.5 left in an arbitrary order), `legacy_edges_in_the_orientation_of_0_8_0`.
