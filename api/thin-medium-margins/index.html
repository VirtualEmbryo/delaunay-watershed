    Thin margins of medium¶

    A thin margin of medium between a cell and the image box: why the cell took it, and the padding that prevents it.

    The defect¶

    Before 0.8.0, a cell separated from a face of the image box by a few voxels of medium could take that medium: at the default min_distance of 3, a 12-voxel cube 4 voxels from every face meshed to 2.98 times its voxel count, and the two boxes of a 24-voxel smoke mask to 2.46 times. The mechanism, measured on cubes of side 8 to 32:

    1. The distance field is 0 on the image box's outer layer of voxels (dw3d.edt._pad_mask makes the box a boundary), which is below the level of the membranes (M - 1, 0.732 on these masks).
    2. A minimum is placed only where no lower voxel lies within min_distance (skimage.feature.peak_local_max's window). So the membrane between the cell and a face loses its minima to the box: at a margin of 2 or 3 voxels it carries none at all, on every cube measured.
    3. With no point on that membrane, the tetrahedra joining the cell's interior point to the box span the medium, and the seeded watershed hands them to the cell through faces scoring like the cell's interior. Even a labelling by tetrahedron centroid would give the cell 1.26 to 1.47 times its voxels: the tetrahedralisation is wrong there, not only its labelling.

    The range grows with min_distance: the defect reached margins of 5 to 7 voxels at min_distance 3, 7 to 8 at 4 and 8 to 10 at 5, and was gone from 2 * min_distance + 2 voxels on (8, 10, 12), on cubes of side 12 to 32. Its signature was exact on those cubes: every cube whose mesh volume exceeded its voxel count by more than 15 % had triangles of the cell on a face of the image box that the cell's voxels do not reach (unsupported border triangles). Real cells are rounded and come close to the box at one place only; of 684 masks (47 benchmark tissues, 141 of the resolution sweep, 492 frames of a real embryo series, the four test images), 6 had unsupported border triangles (1 to 5 each), and the 362 cells within 7 voxels of a face kept a mesh volume of 0.85 to 1.45 times their voxel count, inside the agreement band of dw3d.cell_volume_agreement.

    The fix¶

    needs_padding decides it after mesh surgery, before anything is reported: when a vertex of the mesh lies on a face of the image box that no cell of the mask reaches, the reconstruction is run again on the mask padded with label 0 on every face that no cell reaches and whose margin (its smallest gap to a cell) is below thin_margin_bound(min_distance), each up to that bound (padding_of_thin_margins), and the finished mesh is translated back into the mask's frame. Every cell then sits at least 2 * min_distance + 2 voxels from those faces, as on a mask imaged with a wider margin. The mask's own voxels keep the dither they have unpadded (the default placement adds U(0, 1e-5) from RandomState(42) to the distance field; drawn on the padded array, every voxel would get another value, and the whole mesh would be a new draw): dither_padding of dw3d.points_on_edt._dithered. So only the mesh within the padded faces' reach changes: on the 18 padded masks of the lab's data, 0 to 28 line-mean wedges of 3 to 123 changed on 13 resolution-sweep masks and 4 to 18 of about 700 on 5 embryo frames, where a dither drawn on the padded array changed every wedge. All thin faces are padded, not only the one that triggered: the tetrahedralisation is global, and padding one face of a cube 4 voxels from every face moved the defect to the others (1.13 to 2.68 times the voxel count). A mask whose first mesh has no vertex on a face no cell reaches is meshed exactly as before. A face that a cell reaches is a crop (dw3d.crop_faces) and is never padded: padding it with medium would close the cut cells there. A cell left with unsupported border triangles, on such a face or on a face whose margin is already at the bound, is named by ThinMediumMarginWarning.

    On a padded face the mesh is that of the same tissue imaged with a wider margin, so the cell's surface may reach a few voxels into the medium there, and past the image box when the margin is thinner than that reach; before, the box clipped it and the cell took the medium instead. The overshoot is at most the face's padding (the mesh lies inside the padded box), and was measured at: 7 voxels on a 16-voxel cube 1 voxel from every face, and up to 4 on cubes of side 8 to 24 at margins of 1 to 5 voxels; up to 4 voxels on foambryo's test masks (four cells in a ball 4 voxels from the faces, with cubic voxels); 0 to 2 voxels on the 18 padded masks of the lab's data (one vertex by 1 voxel on one resolution-sweep mask, five vertices by up to 2 voxels on one embryo frame, none on the others). No vertex passes a crop face, which is never padded.

    What the padding does not do: it does not change how the watershed treats small cells. On a ball of four cells cut by the image box, at min_distance 3, the cell that the watershed empties (UnresolvedCellWarning, cause "removed by mesh surgery") is kept only as a fragment of 4 triangles, 0.010 of its voxel volume inside the box of voxel centres; and a cell with one or two interior points (a cube of 8 or 12 voxels, or a cell of that ball) can fall below half its voxel count on some dither draws, with or without padding (2 of 20 seeds for the 8-voxel cube 8 voxels from every face).

    Where it is not applied by default: a mask that construct_mesh_in_physical_units resamples (voxels longer along one axis). On foambryo's four-cell ball 4 voxels from the faces, with z twice as long, over dither seeds 42-141, a cell kept less than half its voxel volume on 20 of 100 seeds padded against 3 of 100 unpadded (McNemar exact p = 1.5e-5; with cubic voxels 13 against 10, p = 0.51). The cell is lost in the watershed: the cell facing a padded face hands most of its interior to the medium through a face straddling its outer membrane, before that interior reaches its seed; the distance field there is the unpadded one, the tetrahedralisation near the padded faces is not, and why this happens more often on resampled masks was not found. Such a mask keeps 0.7's thin-margin meshes, named by ThinMediumMarginWarning; pad_thin_medium_margins=True in that call pads it.

    What the trigger misses: it reads the first mesh's vertices, so a cell that collapses near a face without reaching it is not padded. On cubes of side 6 to 12 voxels 1 to 5 voxels from one or every face, min_distance 3 to 5, ten dither seeds (1 200 runs), 25 collapsed to 0.00-0.49 of their voxels with no vertex on a face; padded, they give 0.58-0.80, and the same cubes 16 voxels from every face 0.51-0.71. dw2d decides from the mask instead (dw2d.thin_medium_margins).

    ThinMediumMarginWarning ¶

    Bases: UserWarning

    A cell's mesh lies on a face of the image box that its voxels do not reach, and padding could not prevent it.

    Emitted once per reconstruction, naming each such cell, the face, the cell's medium margin to it (the number of voxels of label 0 between the cell and the face) and why the face was not padded: a cell reaches it (it is a crop), padding is switched off, or the margin is already thin_margin_bound(min_distance) or more. The cell may have taken some of the medium between it and the face; compare its volume with its voxel count (cell_volume_report). See dw3d.thin_medium_margins.
