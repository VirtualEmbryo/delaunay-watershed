    Cell volumes against the mask¶

    List the cells whose reconstructed mesh volume disagrees with their voxel count.

    Why this exists¶

    A reconstruction can lose a cell or misplace part of it while the mesh stays closed, manifold and free of self-intersections, so no topological check sees it. Measured with the default algorithm at min_distance 3: on the 47 benchmark masks 2 of 240 cells, on two 246-timepoint real embryo series 169 of 5 038, and on a 1 866-cell tissue 20 cells, have a mesh volume below half or above one and a half times their voxel count, a non-positive volume, or no surface at all. Almost all of them are small cells (a few hundred voxels, about two voxels thick) that the seeded watershed never grows out of; one is a single-tetrahedron cell that junction relocation turned inside out. Their interfaces are missing or misplaced, and a pressure inferred for such a cell is not a measurement.

    This module changes no geometry. It compares each label's mesh volume with its voxel count and reports the labels that fall outside a fixed range, so that a user, or foambryo, can see which cells to distrust.

    Conventions¶

    The mesh volume of a label is the divergence-theorem volume with the package's winding convention: +det(p0, p1, p2) / 6 for every triangle whose first label it is, -det / 6 for every triangle whose second label it is. Volumes are in cubic voxels, so the comparison is made on the mesh in mask voxel units, before any pixel-size scaling. Label 0 is the exterior medium and is never listed. The range [0.5, 1.5] is inclusive; its bounds are fixed, not tuning parameters.

    CellVolumeDisagreementWarning ¶

    Bases: UserWarning

    Some cells of the last reconstruction have a mesh volume that disagrees with their voxel count.

    Emitted by :meth:dw3d.reconstruction_algorithm.MeshReconstructionAlgorithm.construct_mesh_from_segmentation_mask once per reconstruction, when :func:compare_cell_volumes_with_mask lists at least one cell. The list is the algorithm's cell_volume_report. The mesh itself is unchanged. The warning is a subclass of :class:UserWarning, so it can be silenced or promoted to an error on its own with :func:warnings.filterwarnings.

    compare_cell_volumes_with_mask ¶

    compare_cell_volumes_with_mask(segmented_mask: NDArray, points: NDArray[float64], triangles: NDArray[integer], labels: NDArray[integer], ratio_range: tuple[float, float] = CELL_VOLUME_RATIO_RANGE) -> CellVolumeReport

    List every non-zero label whose mesh volume disagrees with its voxel count.

    A label is listed when no triangle carries it ("absent"), when its mesh volume is <= 0 ("non-positive"), or when its mesh volume over voxel count is below ("below") or above ("above") the inclusive ratio_range, tested in that order.
