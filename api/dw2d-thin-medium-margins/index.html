    Thin margins of medium (2D)¶

    A thin margin of medium between a cell and the image border: why the cell took it, and the padding that prevents it.

    The same defect as in dw3d (dw3d.thin_medium_margins), and the same rule.

    The defect¶

    dw2d's distance field is 0 on the image's outer ring of pixels (dw2d.Geometric_utilities.pad_mask makes the image border a boundary), at or below the level of the membranes, and a membrane point is placed only where no lower value lies within min_distance (skimage.feature.peak_local_max). So the membrane between a cell and a side of the image a few pixels away loses its points, the triangles joining the cell's interior to the border span the medium, and the watershed gives them to the cell. Measured before 0.8.0 on single squares (side 12, 20, 32 px), squares with the margin on one side, a doublet of 20-px squares and disks (radius 10 and 16 px), at min_distance 3, 4 and 5: at margins of 2 px up to about min_distance, the membrane facing the near side carried no point, the cell had edges on a side its pixels do not reach, and its area was 1.04 to 1.73 times its pixel count; from min_distance + 1 or + 2 px on, every cell was back at 0.84 to 0.99 (its value at a margin of 16 px).

    The fix¶

    dw2d places its points on the field without a random dither, so the mask can be padded before it is meshed: every side of the image that no cell reaches and whose margin (its smallest gap to a cell) is below thin_margin_bound(min_distance), 2 * min_distance + 2 as in dw3d, is padded with label 0 up to that bound, and the mesh is translated back into the mask's frame. The pixels of the mask keep the field and the points they have unpadded, except within the padded sides' reach. A mask whose cells reach every side or stay at least that far from it is meshed exactly as before. A side that a cell reaches is a crop and is never padded. A cell left with edges on a side its pixels do not reach is named by ThinMediumMarginWarning. On a padded side the mesh is that of the same tissue imaged with a wider margin, so a cell's boundary may reach past the image border there, by at most the padding.

    dw3d decides from a first mesh (a vertex on a face no cell reaches), because its meshes cost more and its dither makes a padded mesh a new draw. In 2D that test would miss the defect twice over: at a margin of min_distance the cell took the medium without reaching the side (1.28 times its pixels at min_distance 4, its nearest vertex 2.5 px beyond the membrane), and a 12-px square 3 or 4 px from one side collapsed to a triangle of 0.23 times its pixels, with no vertex near the side, at min_distance 5 (0.84 at a margin of 12 px).

    thin_margin_bound ¶

    thin_margin_bound(min_distance: int) -> int

    The medium margin, in pixels, below which a side no cell reaches is padded: 2 * min_distance + 2 (dw3d's).

    cells_on_each_image_side ¶

    cells_on_each_image_side(mask: NDArray[integer]) -> tuple[tuple[int, ...], ...]

    The cells (non-zero labels) with a pixel on each side of the image, in the order of IMAGE_SIDES.

    side_margins ¶

    side_margins(mask: NDArray[integer]) -> tuple[int | None, ...]

    The medium margin of each side: the pixels of label 0 between it and the nearest cell along its normal.

    Returns:

    Type Description
    tuple[int | None, ...]

    tuple[int | None, ...]: in the order of IMAGE_SIDES; 0 when a cell reaches the side, None without a cell.

    padding_of_thin_margins ¶

    padding_of_thin_margins(margins: tuple[int | None, ...], cells_on_sides: tuple[tuple[int, ...], ...], bound: int) -> tuple[tuple[int, int], tuple[int, int]] | None

    The numpy.pad widths that bring every side no cell reaches to a margin of bound pixels, or None.

    unsupported_border_edges(points: NDArray[floating], segments: NDArray[integer], labels: NDArray[integer], mask: NDArray[integer]) -> list[tuple[int, str]]

    (cell, side) for each cell with an edge lying on a side of the image that its pixels do not reach.
