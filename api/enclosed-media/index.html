    Enclosed media¶

    Pockets of medium that the tissue encloses: their own regions, flagged as media, instead of part of a cell.

    Why this exists¶

    The watershed places one seed per label value, at the label's largest EDT value. The medium, label 0, is one value, so before 0.7.1 it had one seed, in whichever of its pieces was deepest. An enclosed pocket of label 0 (a lumen, a cavity) had none: its tetrahedra formed one unseeded group, which the first seeded cell it reached took whole. On a 697-cell embryo with a 389 918-voxel lumen, one lining cell came out at 2.40 times its voxel volume, and no lining cell kept a membrane against the lumen. When a pocket was deeper than the exterior (a tight crop), label 0's seed fell in the pocket and the exterior was absorbed instead.

    What it does¶

    Pieces of label 0 are 6-connected (two voxels sharing a face), as foambryo's mask report counts them. A piece touching no face of the array is an enclosed pocket. A pocket becomes its own region, flagged as a medium, when its inscribed radius is at least min_distance + 1 voxels and at least two cells line it:

    • the inscribed radius is the largest Euclidean distance, in voxels, from a pocket voxel to the nearest voxel outside the pocket. Below about 3 voxels at min_distance 3 a pocket holds no tetrahedron, and a seed placed in it would take a neighbouring cell's tetrahedron instead, so it is left as before;
    • a pocket lined by one cell is a hole in that cell's segmentation, not a lumen between cells: as its own region it would be a closed surface with no triple line, whose tension no method can infer. It is left as before.

    The new labels are max + 1, max + 2, ..., in the order of each pocket's lowest raster (C-order) index. Pockets left as before are reported, and EnclosedMediumWarning names the cell that took each.

    Every piece of label 0 that touches the image box is exterior and gets its own label-0 seed (see dw3d.reconstruction_algorithm). A mask with no pocket at or above the bound and one exterior piece is reconstructed bitwise as before.

    Herve Turlier's group, 2026.

    UnresolvedCellWarning ¶

    Bases: UserWarning

    Some regions of the mask have no tetrahedron of their own and are absent from the mesh.

    A region's watershed seed is the tetrahedron whose centroid is nearest to the region's deepest voxel. When no tetrahedron centroid lies in the region's voxels (a cell a few voxels thick, or smaller than the point placement's min_distance), that tetrahedron belongs to a neighbour; before 0.7.1 the region grew from it inside the neighbour, as a phantom. Since 0.7.1 the seed is dropped and the region is absent from the mesh. Emitted once per reconstruction that drops a seed, naming each region, its voxel count and the min_distance; the regions are the reconstruction's enclosed_medium_report.unresolved. A smaller min_distance may resolve them.

    EnclosedMediumWarning ¶

    Bases: UserWarning

    Enclosed pockets of medium were left to the cells around them.

    Emitted once per reconstruction that leaves at least one pocket of label 0 to the cells (below the size bound, or lined by one cell). The pockets are the reconstruction's enclosed_medium_report.pockets_left_to_cells. A subclass of :class:UserWarning, so it can be silenced or promoted to an error on its own with :func:warnings.filterwarnings.

    changes_the_mesh property ¶

    changes_the_mesh: bool

    Whether any of the three rules acted, so that the mesh may differ from 0.7.0's on the same tesselation.

    False means: no enclosed medium made, label 0 seeded as before, every seed already inside its label. The reconstruction is then bitwise what it was (pockets left to the cells are left as before).
