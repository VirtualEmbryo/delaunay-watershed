# Anisotropic voxels and crop faces

A mask with voxels twice as long along z, resampled to cubic voxels and meshed in physical units (`construct_mesh_in_physical_units`), what meshing the native grid does instead, and a mask cut by the image box: crop faces, cut cells, the `.rec` file that keeps them, and a face declared physical.

Data: nothing: the mask is generated in the notebook.

The notebook: [`Examples/example_6_anisotropic_voxels_and_crop_faces.ipynb`](https://github.com/VirtualEmbryo/delaunay-watershed/blob/v0.7.0/Examples/example_6_anisotropic_voxels_and_crop_faces.ipynb).
